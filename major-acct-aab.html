Accounting

About 2 yearsAbout 60 credits, full-time
TrumbullWhere you'd take it

What it is

Bookkeeping, payroll, taxes, and financial records, the money side of every business.

You might like it if…

  • You like when numbers add up
  • You're detail-oriented
  • You want steady work that every business needs

Heads up

  • Becoming a CPA later needs a bachelor's and more
  • Lots of careful, repetitive detail work

Jobs it can lead to

Bookkeeper
Accounts payable or payroll clerk
Accounting assistant

Pay shown only where we have real numbers. National medians include people with years of experience. See real bookkeeper job posts near Warren to check starting pay.

How it fits you

A fast route to an office job. Many credits can carry into a business bachelor's later, so ask an advisor.

What it costs

About $15,700 in tuition full-time before aid (4 semesters at the lower-division rate). Max Pell alone covers almost all of it.
