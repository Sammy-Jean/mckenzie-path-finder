What it is

Training to become a registered nurse: anatomy, patient care, medications, and lots of hands-on clinical hours in hospitals.

You might like it if…

  • You want to help people in a very direct way
  • You stay calm when things get intense
  • You're okay with science classes and long shifts

Heads up

  • Admission to nursing programs is usually selective, so grades in science and math matter a lot
  • You'll need to pass a licensing exam after graduating

Jobs it can lead to

Registered nurse
Later: specialty nursing, school nurse, nurse educator

Pay shown only where we have real numbers. National medians include people with years of experience. See real registered nurse job posts near Warren to check starting pay.

How it fits you

Calm under pressure is huge in nursing. ASL is valuable in hospitals, where communicating with Deaf patients is a real need.

What it costs

About $33,400 in tuition full-time before aid. With max Pell, SEOG, and Trumbull's 2.5 GPA scholarship, most of that is covered.
